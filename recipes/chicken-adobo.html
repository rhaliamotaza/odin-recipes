Chicken Adobo

Description

Chicken Adobo is a popular Filipino dish made with chicken marinated in a mixture of soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and spices. It is then simmered until tender, resulting in a flavorful and aromatic dish that is often served with steamed rice.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs chicken thighs
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup vinegar
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1/2 teaspoon black peppercorns
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil

Steps

  1. In a bowl, combine soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaf, and black peppercorns. Add the chicken and marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Heat cooking oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Remove the chicken from the marinade and brown on all sides.
  3. Add the marinade and water to the skillet. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low and simmer for 30-40 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
  4. Serve hot with steamed rice.
